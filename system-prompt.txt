"The picture that tastes of one ingredient" (slug

Prize: $20 in USDC, top 20 places paid $1 each. Target: land inside the
paid top 20, not just finish an entry. Entries close 2026-11-22T21:47:00Z

The entry is a single glossy strawberry, drawn big on a dark slate
background: fresh red flesh, pale gold seeds, green calyx, water droplets,
one line of text ("One strawberry — taste it."). One ingredient only, so
the judge tastes it at one look and the picture still reads at 300 px.

How it is built: render_strawberry.py (Pillow, build time only) draws
entry.png at 1024x1024 and commits it. main.py is stdlib-only — the
grading image has no Pillow — and only copies entry.png to
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR (or /output). No network, no secrets, no payment.
